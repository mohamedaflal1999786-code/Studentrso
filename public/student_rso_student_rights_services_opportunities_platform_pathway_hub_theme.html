THE Challenge

Good opportunities exist. Finding them is the hard part.

Students often navigate information across multiple portals, notices and sources. Understanding eligibility, finding the right service, completing an application and identifying reliable information can be unnecessarily difficult.

01 / ELIGIBILITY

Complex Eligibility Requirements

Scholarships, examinations and courses often have different eligibility requirements, making it difficult for students to know what they qualify for.

rule Unclear eligibility criteria
02 / DEADLINES

Scattered Deadlines

Important dates can be spread across official portals, notices and institutional announcements, making them easy to miss.

schedule Missed application dates
03 / RIGHTS & COMPLAINTS

Unclear Support Pathways

Students may not know where to turn when they face safety concerns, unfair treatment or other issues requiring formal support.

lock Unclear reporting channels
04 / TRUST

Unverified Information

Information from unofficial websites, social media and informal sources can be outdated, incomplete or difficult to verify.

warning Unchecked circulars
05 / VISIBILITY

Limited Application Visibility

Students may have difficulty knowing where an application, certificate request or reported concern stands after it has been submitted.

visibility_off Limited status visibility
06 / GUIDANCE

Unclear Education Pathways

Students may struggle to understand which courses, institutions and career pathways best match their interests, academic background, budget and goals.
